Every case, five stages

What I do there

  1. 01

    Discovery meeting

    I sit down with the office and ask what the chore really is.

  2. 02

    Spec

    I write down what the tool will do and what it must never touch.

  3. 03

    Build

    I build with the case team, one small pull request at a time.

  4. 04

    Review

    I review teammates’ code, and they review mine, before anything merges.

  5. 05

    Delivery meeting

    I hand the tool over and write down what to fix next.

How a case runs

  • “Every case goes through the same five stages, and my job changes at each one.”
  • “Most of my term was the first and last rows: meetings, not code.”
  • “Review is the one I’ll come back to.”
